** Surprises and decisions
Three findings drove the design and are worth not rediscovering.

=TEFUNC= is not a pure function of =(t, y)=: it advances the disturbance walks,
draws measurement noise, ticks the sampled analyzers, and latches valve sticking
on every call. Harmless under fixed-step Euler, silently wrong under any
multi-stage integrator. Hence the pure/impure split in the plan.

The RNG is bit-reproducible. =G = DMOD(G*9228907, 4294967296)= overflows 2^53, so
rounding is part of the algorithm, but IEEE fixes both the multiply and the
=fmod=. The existing Python port's noise divergence is therefore a *call-ordering*
defect, not an arithmetic one, which is why Tier 3 (RNG call-trace diff) exists
and is cheap.

Exact long-horizon trajectory matching against gfortran is impossible because
=exp= and =pow= differ by ULPs and the closed loop amplifies that. Tier 4 is
diagnostic only. The real gates are Tier 5 (equivalence testing on means *and
variances*, since the Python port's failure mode was variance collapse: reactor
pressure sigma 8.10 against Fortran's 61.48) and Tier 6 (cross-source detector
performance).

Four scoping decisions recorded in =BACKLOG.org= under Decisions.

** Surprises and decisions
The upstream license is *not* BSD-3-Clause, despite the upstream README saying
so. The actual text is the University of Illinois/NCSA Open Source License: an
MIT-style grant carrying three BSD-style conditions, copyright 1998-2002 The
Board of Trustees of the University of Illinois, developed by the Large Scale
Systems Research Laboratory under Prof. Richard D. Braatz.

This has a practical consequence that is easy to miss: the NCSA conditions
require the notice and disclaimers to be retained in redistributions in *binary*
form, not only source. So =LICENSE-NCSA= and =NOTICE.md= must be packaged into
the Python wheels and the wasm bundle in Phase 7 and Phase 8. Recorded here so
that is not discovered at publish time.

=LICENSE-NCSA= is verbatim including its typographical corruption (stray =%=
characters in the disclaimer, irregular whitespace). Deliberate: a license gets
carried as distributed, not tidied.

New-code license is BSD-3-Clause, matching the upstream Python project. The
crates.io norm is dual MIT OR Apache-2.0 for the patent grant. Left open in the
backlog; cheap to change before first publish.

Protocol bug, found by executing it: the close-out asked for *one* commit per
iteration *and* for =:COMMIT:= to record that commit's hash. Impossible, since a
commit cannot contain its own hash. Amending to fix the recorded value just
changes the hash again. =CLAUDE.md= now specifies two commits per iteration, the
work commit followed by a bookkeeping commit that names it, with =pending= as the
placeholder in between, and forbids =--amend= after a hash has been recorded.
