* Phase 2: the plant model

Decompose into iteration-sized items when starting this phase. The intended
shape, in dependency order:

- State and Derivative types with a =VectorSpace= trait; flat view for interop
  only, never the working representation
- Splitting =TEFUNC= into a pure =derivatives()= and an impure
  =advance_discrete()= (see =PLAN.org=, "Splitting the right-hand side"). This
  is the load-bearing structural decision; get it right before porting bodies.
- Vapor-liquid equilibrium and pressures for reactor, separator, mixing zone
- Reaction kinetics: four reactions, Arrhenius, fractional pressure orders
- Stream network: valve-lagged flows, square-root resistances, compressor curve
- Heat transfer: reactor coil with level and agitator dependence, condenser
  saturating coefficient, stripper steam duty
- Stripper stripping factors, including both temperature breakpoints
- Balance assembly into all 50 derivatives
- Tier 2 harness plus the adversarial state catalog, enumerated from the source:
  reactor level at the 10 and 50 percent heat-transfer breakpoints, stripper
  temperature at 5.292 and 170 C, both compressor pressure-ratio clamps, every
  =DLP < 0= sign flip, separator underflow near the 0.1 branch threshold, and
  states tripping each of the eight shutdown conditions
- Tier 2 acceptance at 1e-12 across all three sampling pools
