*Transcription hazard, found in B-0004b:* 182 of the constant assignments in
=TEINIT= are written without a =D= suffix, making them *single precision*
literals that are widened to double. =XMW(2)=25.4= at =teprob.f:942= actually
stores 25.399999618530273. Writing =25.4_f64= in Rust is wrong by 1.5e-8
relative, five orders of magnitude past the Tier 1 tolerance of 1e-13. Each
constant must be transcribed according to whether its literal carries a =D=
suffix, and the choice must be visible in the source rather than implicit.
