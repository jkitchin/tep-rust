           the algebra, and it is not present in the configuration that is
           bit-exact, but it is outside the equivalence margin.
:RESULT: *A stuck valve is judged on its distribution, not on its mean.*
         Signed off 2026-08-28. =teprob.f:793-798= widens a valve's dead band
         under IDV(14), IDV(15) and IDV(19), and =teprob.f:801= then moves that
         valve only when =DABS(VCV(I)-XMV(I)).GT.VST(I)*IVST(I)=: a
         discontinuous branch on a floating-point comparison. One ULP of =exp=,
         which Tier 2 accepts on ~10% of arguments, decides which side it lands
         on and the valve then holds a different position for the rest of the
         run. The series is plateaux, so its 48-hour mean is an artefact of how
         long it sat on each one. =Report::failures= now skips the mean and
         variance gates for those variables and relies on the four calibrated
         distributional statistics, which already ran and still run.
         *No margin was widened and no tolerance loosened*: every other
         variable is judged exactly as before, and
         =a_stuck_valve_is_still_judged_on_its_distribution= injects a
         ten-sigma shift into XMV(10) under IDV(14) and requires KS and energy
         distance to exceed every within-source split. Implemented in B-0067.
:END:

Of twenty-one scenarios, nineteen pass every gate. Two do not, and they are
=IDV(14)= and =IDV(19)=. The channels affected are *precisely the valves those
faults stick*:

| scenario | paired power | undecided channels        | worst gap | frobenius |
|----------+--------------+---------------------------+-----------+-----------|
| IDV(14)  |       1.4271 | XMV(10), XMV(11), XMV(5)  | 1.114 mar | 3.002     |
| IDV(19)  |       1.2117 | XMV(5)                    | 0.702 mar | 2.176     |

=teprob.f:793-796= maps them: =IVST(10) = IDV(14)=, =IVST(5,7,8,9) = IDV(19)=.

*The cause is established, not guessed.* =teprob.f:801= moves a valve only when
the command differs from its current position by more than the stick threshold:

: IF(TIME.EQ.0 .OR. DABS(VCV(I)-XMV(I)).GT.VST(I)*IVST(I)) VCV(I)=XMV(I)

That is a discontinuous branch on a floating-point comparison. Under the
vendored libm the two implementations differ by an ULP or so, and sooner or
later that puts them on opposite sides of the threshold: one valve moves and
the other does not, for a whole control period, and the difference becomes
macroscopic. Measured at 1.016e-1 relative on =XMV(10)= at sample 31 of a
2-hour =IDV(14)= run.

*Under =libm-system= all five tested scenarios, these two included, are
bit-identical: 0.000e0.* So there is nothing here but transcendental rounding
meeting a discrete decision. Same mechanism as the strict-comparison finding of
B-0021 and B-0022.

The margin was *not* widened and the port was *not* changed. What needs
deciding is which of these the project wants to claim:

1. Accept it, and state the equivalence claim as holding for the eighteen
   scenarios without a sticking valve, plus bit-exactness under =libm-system=
   for all twenty-one.
2. Ship =libm-system= as the default, which would make the claim unconditional
   and cost cross-platform determinism, a hard invariant. Almost certainly the
   wrong trade.
3. Exclude the manipulated variables of a stuck valve from the mean test on
   the grounds that a bimodal variable has no meaningful mean, and gate them
   on the distribution statistics only, which already pass at p = 1.0000.

Option 3 is the most defensible and is a change to what is measured rather than
to a threshold, but it is a change to the equivalence claim and so needs a
decision.

** 2026-08-28 Tier 5's mean test is paired, and the seed count stays at 100
:PROPERTIES:
:ITEM: B-0047c
:END:

B-0047b measured that =PLAN.org='s margin of a tenth of a standard deviation on
the mean needs about 255 seeds at the nominal plant, not the 100 the plan
specifies. The partial full battery put it between 237 and 460 depending on
scenario, worst at =IDV(6)=.

The backlog offered three ways out: run 255 seeds, keep 100 and record the
manipulated variables as undecided, or argue the margin is the wrong yardstick.
There is a fourth, and it is better than all of them.

*The two sources are run at the same seeds.* Each seed's difference is
therefore an observation, and the seed-to-seed wander cancels out of it
entirely, because both sources are given the same disturbance realisation and
see the same wander. The unpaired test spends almost all of its variance on a
term that is common to both sides.

That is why the power was so poor and why more seeds was the wrong answer. The
variables that ran out of power are the manipulated ones, driven by integrating
controllers whose 48-hour mean has a random-walk component. The two
implementations differ by far less than that walk moves.

*Decision: gate on the paired test, keep 100 seeds, and report the unpaired
result alongside.* Measured on a fixture with a shared offset of order one and
a true difference of 0.01: unpaired power 7.75, which cannot resolve it at any
difference, against paired power 0.0000. Against a source compared with itself
the paired power is exactly zero even at four seeds.

The unpaired result is kept because it is the conservative statement and is the
right one for any comparison that cannot be seed-matched. Nothing is hidden:
both appear in the report and =Report::undecided= still names anything the gate
could not decide.

This does not touch the calibrated statistics. Kolmogorov-Smirnov, the energy
distance, the autocorrelation, the spectra and the Frobenius norm compare
pooled samples or whole ensembles and were never affected by the pairing.

** 2026-08-28 A validation-only statistics crate, tepsim-stats
:PROPERTIES:
:ITEM: B-0042
:END:

=PLAN.org='s workspace layout has no home for the Tier 5, 6 and 7 machinery.
The plan lists =xtask/= as "build, validation-report, release automation", and
one reading is that the statistics belong there.

They do not, for one concrete reason: the Tier 5 differentials are *tests*, in
=tepsim-oracle=, and a test crate cannot sensibly depend on =xtask=. The
statistics have two consumers, the report generator and the differential
tests, and the only shape that serves both is a library crate.

*Decision: add =crates/tepsim-stats=, validation-only.* No shipped crate may
depend on it, and =xtask ci= asserts that, exactly as it already does for
=tepsim-oracle=. Unlike the oracle it needs no gfortran, so it builds and runs
everywhere, including wasm.

It is =no_std + alloc= and =forbid(unsafe_code)= like the rest, and
deterministic under the same rules: no =f32=, no reordered reductions, fixed
summation order.

The alternative of putting the statistics in =tepsim-oracle= was rejected
because half of Tier 5 (the physics invariants, and every port-versus-port
comparison such as the D-011 delta) has nothing to do with the Fortran and
should not require gfortran to run.

=PLAN.org= updated: the layout gains the crate, and the isolation rule now
names both.

** 2026-08-27 Tier 2 measures against the scale of the terms
:PROPERTIES:
:ITEM: B-0026a
:END:

=PLAN.org= set Tier 2 at "rel err < 1e-12", read as relative to the derivative.
B-0025 measured that 28 of the 50 components cannot meet it, and established
that the port is not the reason: under =libm-system= the whole right-hand side
is bit-identical to gfortran, 35,850 comparisons, 0 ULP.

The cause is cancellation. A balance is inflow minus outflow, those nearly
agree near steady state, and one ULP in each term is 1e-16 of the terms and up
to 1e-4 of the result. The 22 components that *do* meet 1e-12 are exactly the
ones that do not cancel.

*Decision: measure against the scale of the terms.* Each balance reports the
magnitude of the largest term entering it, and the gate is the error over that.
Same 1e-12, asked of a quantity that can answer it.

Two candidates were rejected. Keeping relative-to-result and gating only the
non-cancelling components leaves 28 of 50 ungated, which is most of the model.
Making bit equality under =libm-system= the gate is the strongest claim
available but moves Tier 2 off the configuration that actually ships.

=PLAN.org= updated. B-0026 implements it.

Append-only record of choices that changed =PLAN.org=.

** 2026-08-11 The RHS split is three phases, not two
=PLAN.org= described =TEFUNC= as splitting into a pure =derivatives()= and one
impure =advance_discrete()= returning =Measurements=. Enumerating the impure
sites by line number in B-0012 showed that cannot work as written: the
measurement vector is built at =teprob.f:679-701= from flows the pure
evaluation computes, so the noise and analyzer work at =711-761= can only
happen *after* the derivative, while the walk advance at =359-396= must happen
*before* it. One impure call cannot sit on both sides.

The split is therefore =advance_discrete= (pre), =derivatives= (pure, also
returning the noise-free signals), =sample_measurements= (post). The valve
command latch at =793-804= is hoisted into the pre-phase, which is numerically
free because nothing in =345-792= writes =IDV=, =XMV=, =VCV=, =VST= or =IVST=.
B-0014 proves that by scanning the vendored source, and the scan found the
range in this entry was originally off by one.

The pure/impure distinction the plan called for is unchanged and so is the
reason for it. Only the number of impure entry points moved.
